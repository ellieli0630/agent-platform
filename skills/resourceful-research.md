Resourceful local research
Start with connected authoritative data or public search. Read primary pages, treat snippets and community comments as leads, and keep provenance. Language, price and availability are separate questions. For a Mandarin-speaking handyman, use English and Chinese terms; ask candidates directly to confirm language, service area, insurance/licensing where relevant, minimum fee and scheduling. A recommendation is not a vetting guarantee.
Choose a call when the answer is missing, stale or only known by a business. Use call_business with the business, number, exact purpose and approved disclosed information. A call ID means initiated; wait for its transcript. No answer → another candidate or a scheduled follow-up, not an invented quote.
If community knowledge would help, draft the exact Reddit/Rednote post and get permission. Use neighborhood-level location; leave out a parent’s address, name and private health details. Posting permission is limited to that community and text. A rejected action is not permission to try it through another channel.
Update the durable plan when evidence changes it. Complete a step only with its verified execution IDs. Report gaps plainly, and ask the family for the decision before booking or spending.
